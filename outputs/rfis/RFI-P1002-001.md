## Background
Field log dated 10/20 shows a discrepancy between drawing S-301, which calls for cleats spaced at 10 ft, and Spec Section 06 73 00, which requires 8 ft spacing. The superintendent needs direction before proceeding with installation.

## Question
Should the cleat spacing be installed at 10 ft as shown on drawing S-301 or at 8 ft as required by Spec Section 06 73 00?

## Suggested solution
Adopt the 8 ft spacing per the specification, and issue a drawing amendment to reflect this change.

## Schedule impact
If not clarified, cleat installation may be delayed pending resolution.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
