## Background
During installation on 10/20, Superintendent Mike R observed a conflict between the contract drawing and the specification for cleat spacing. The drawing S-301 shows a 10 ft spacing, while Spec Section 06 73 00 requires an 8 ft spacing.

## Question
Should the installed cleats follow the 8 ft spacing required by Spec Section 06 73 00 or the 10 ft spacing shown on Drawing S-301?

## Suggested solution
Adopt the 8 ft spacing per the specification and issue a revised detail if needed.

## Schedule impact
If not clarified, cleat installation cannot proceed, delaying the deck work schedule.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
