## Background
During field observation on 10/20, Superintendent Ana G noted that the impact windows submittal does not meet the required project specifications. The current submittal lacks the necessary performance data and material details. This discrepancy prevents the windows from being approved for installation.

## Question
Can the architect provide an approved revised impact windows submittal that meets the project specifications?

## Suggested solution
Submit a revised shop drawing set and product data sheet reflecting the required impact resistance rating and frame details.

## Schedule impact
The window installation is on the critical path and cannot proceed until the revised submittal is approved, potentially delaying the façade schedule.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
