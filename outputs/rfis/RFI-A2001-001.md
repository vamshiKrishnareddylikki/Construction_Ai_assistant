## Background
During installation on 10/20, Superintendent Mike R noted a discrepancy between the cleat spacing shown on drawing S-301 (10 ft) and the requirement in spec section 06 73 00 (8 ft). The conflict must be resolved before the cleats are installed to avoid rework.

## Question
Should the cleat spacing follow the 10 ft dimension shown on drawing S-301 or the 8 ft requirement in spec section 06 73 00?

## Suggested solution
Adopt the 8 ft spacing per the specification, as it is the controlling document, unless the architect issues a drawing revision.

## Schedule impact
If not clarified, installation will be delayed pending a decision, potentially impacting the overall project schedule.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
