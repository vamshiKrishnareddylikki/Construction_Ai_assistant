## Background
During construction on 10/20, the field log noted rotted floor joists in the medical fitout area. The superintendent, Dave K, has requested a structural engineer's review to determine the appropriate remedial action.

## Question
Can the structural engineer confirm whether the existing joists can be repaired or must be replaced, and provide the required design details?

## Suggested solution
If repair is not feasible, replace the affected joists with new members sized per the structural engineer's calculations, using the same material and grade as the original design.

## Schedule impact
Work cannot proceed in the affected area until the joist condition is resolved, potentially delaying the project schedule.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
