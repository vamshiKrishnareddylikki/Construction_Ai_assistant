## Background
During field observation on 10/20, the superintendent identified rotted floor joists beneath the sink area that are not shown on the current drawings. The condition requires a structural engineer’s inspection before any repair or replacement can proceed.

## Question
Can we proceed with a structural engineer’s inspection and receive approval for the necessary joist repair/replacement as soon as possible?

## Suggested solution
Engage a structural engineer to assess the damaged joists, design appropriate reinforcement or replacement, and allow the contractor to replace the rotted members per the engineer’s recommendations.

## Schedule impact
The work in the sink area cannot continue until the joists are inspected and approved, potentially delaying downstream trades.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
