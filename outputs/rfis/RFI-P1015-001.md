## Background
During the renovation of Project P1015, the field crew observed rotted floor joists that were not indicated on the current drawings. The superintendent, Dave K, reported the condition on 10/20 and indicated that a structural engineer’s input is needed before proceeding.

## Question
Can the architect provide the structural engineer’s review and required design for the rotted floor joists?

## Suggested solution
Engage a qualified structural engineer to assess the rotted joists and develop a reinforcement or replacement design, then submit the design for architect approval.

## Schedule impact
Floor framing work cannot continue until the structural solution is approved, potentially delaying the project schedule.

*This RFI does not authorize additional cost or time. Any cost or schedule change requires a Change Order.*
