# Sample Subcontract: General Conditions
*Fictional sample document for the Construction AI Assistant project. Company: Coastline Builders LLC.*

## 1. Schedule and Delays
1.1 The Subcontractor shall perform the Work in accordance with the Project Schedule issued by Coastline Builders.
1.2 **Weather delays.** Delays caused by named storms, hurricanes, or tropical storm warnings issued by the National Hurricane Center for the project's county are excusable delays. The Subcontractor is entitled to a time extension equal to the number of working days lost, but not additional compensation.
1.3 To claim a weather delay, the Subcontractor must submit written notice within **7 calendar days** of the start of the delay, including daily reports showing the lost days.
1.4 Normal rain is not an excusable delay unless rainfall exceeds the 10-year monthly average for that month, as recorded by the nearest NOAA weather station.

## 2. Change Orders
2.1 No change to the Work shall be performed without a written Change Order signed by the Coastline Builders Project Manager.
2.2 The Subcontractor shall submit a change order proposal within **10 business days** of receiving a change request, including labor, material, equipment, and markup.
2.3 Markup on change order work is limited to **10% overhead and 5% profit**.
2.4 Work performed without a signed Change Order will not be paid.

## 3. Payment
3.1 Pay applications are due on the **25th of each month** for work completed through the end of that month.
3.2 Retainage of **10%** is held on each payment until the project reaches 50% completion, after which retainage is reduced to **5%**.
3.3 Final retainage is released after closeout documents are received and approved (see Closeout Procedures).

## 4. Insurance
4.1 The Subcontractor shall maintain general liability insurance of at least **$1,000,000 per occurrence** and $2,000,000 aggregate.
4.2 Certificates of insurance must name Coastline Builders LLC and the Owner as additional insured.
