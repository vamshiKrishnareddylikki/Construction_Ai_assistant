# RFI and Submittal Process
*Fictional sample document for the Construction AI Assistant project. Company: Coastline Builders LLC.*

## 1. Requests for Information (RFIs)
- An RFI is submitted when drawings or specifications are unclear, conflicting, or missing information.
- Each RFI must include: project name, RFI number, date, the drawing or spec section referenced, a clear question, and a suggested solution if possible.
- RFIs are submitted through the project management system, never by text message.
- The architect's standard response time is **5 business days**. Urgent RFIs that hold up work must be marked "Critical" and are answered within **2 business days**.
- An RFI response does not authorize extra cost. If the answer changes cost or time, a Change Order request must follow.

## 2. Submittals
- Submittals are shop drawings, product data, and samples sent to the architect for approval **before** materials are ordered or installed.
- Subcontractors submit to Coastline Builders, which reviews them and forwards them to the architect within **3 business days**.
- Architect review time is **10 business days**.
- Possible responses: Approved, Approved as Noted, Revise and Resubmit, or Rejected.
- Materials may not be ordered until the submittal is Approved or Approved as Noted.

## 3. Long-Lead Items
- Items with lead times over 8 weeks (for example, impact windows, elevators, switchgear, and marine pilings) must be submitted within the first **30 days** of the subcontract.
- Florida projects require impact-rated windows and doors with a valid Florida Product Approval or Miami-Dade NOA in the submittal.
