# Project Closeout Procedures
*Fictional sample document for the Construction AI Assistant project. Company: Coastline Builders LLC.*

## 1. Substantial Completion
- Substantial completion is reached when the Owner can occupy or use the project for its intended purpose.
- The architect issues a Certificate of Substantial Completion with an attached **punch list** of remaining items.
- Warranties begin on the date of substantial completion unless stated otherwise.

## 2. Punch List
- The punch list is a list of minor items to finish or fix (paint touch-ups, missing hardware, adjustments).
- Subcontractors must complete their punch list items within **30 days** of substantial completion.
- The project manager tracks punch list status weekly until every item is closed.

## 3. Required Closeout Documents
Each subcontractor must submit:
1. As-built drawings showing actual installed conditions
2. Operation and maintenance (O&M) manuals for all installed equipment
3. Written warranties for materials and workmanship
4. Final lien waivers from the subcontractor and all suppliers
5. Attic stock (spare materials) as listed in the specifications
6. Training sign-off sheets showing the Owner's staff were trained on equipment

## 4. Final Payment
- Final payment and release of retainage occur only after **all** closeout documents are received and approved.
- Missing lien waivers are the most common cause of delayed final payment.

## 5. Post-Construction Warranty Period
- The standard warranty period is **1 year** from substantial completion.
- Roofing systems carry a **10-year** manufacturer warranty.
- Warranty claims are logged by the Owner and assigned to the responsible subcontractor, who must respond within **5 business days**.
- An 11-month walkthrough is scheduled with the Owner to catch issues before the warranty expires.
