# Site Safety Manual (Summary)
*Fictional sample document for the Construction AI Assistant project. Company: Coastline Builders LLC.*

## 1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Hard hats, safety glasses, high-visibility vests, and closed-toe work boots are required at all times on site.
- Hearing protection is required when operating or working near equipment above 85 decibels.
- Gloves appropriate to the task are required when handling materials, rebar, or chemicals.

## 2. Fall Protection
- Fall protection is required for any work **6 feet or more** above a lower level.
- Acceptable systems: guardrails, safety nets, or personal fall arrest systems (full-body harness with a shock-absorbing lanyard).
- Ladders must extend at least **3 feet** above the landing surface.
- Scaffolds must be inspected by a competent person before each shift.

## 3. Heat Illness Prevention (South Florida)
- From May through October, supervisors must provide water, shade, and scheduled rest breaks.
- When the heat index exceeds **91°F**, workers take a 10-minute shade break every hour.
- New workers follow a 5-day acclimatization schedule, starting at 20% of a normal workload.
- Signs of heat stroke (confusion, hot dry skin, fainting) require calling 911 immediately.

## 4. Hurricane Preparedness
- When a hurricane watch is issued for the project's county, the superintendent begins the site securing checklist.
- Within 48 hours of expected landfall: secure or remove loose materials, lower crane booms per manufacturer guidance, remove debris, and secure portable toilets and dumpsters.
- All work stops when a hurricane warning is issued. Workers do not return until the superintendent declares the site safe.

## 5. Incident Reporting
- All injuries, near misses, and property damage must be reported to the superintendent **immediately**.
- A written incident report is due within **24 hours**.
- Serious injuries (hospitalization, amputation, loss of an eye) must be reported to OSHA within **24 hours**; fatalities within **8 hours**.
